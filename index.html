The PhD02 / 06

Where accounting meets analytics and AI.

My doctoral focus is how firms measure and disclose hard-to-value information: digital assets, ESG performance and AI-generated outputs. And how investors, auditors and managers make judgments when that information is uncertain.

The idea03 / 06

When a number is hard to value, its reliability matters as much as the number.

Digital assetsWhich market and price should a firm trust when measuring crypto at fair value? My published CFV-RS score answers this.
ESGDo firms’ sustainability words match their numbers, and does mandatory disclosure change how markets react?
Verifiable AICan AI outputs be explained and audited before people rely on them? SmartProof is my first step.

How I work05 / 06

Start from a real reporting problem. Build the evidence myself.

I begin with questions practitioners actually face, then build the data from the ground up. Most recently, that meant a multi-country ESG panel spanning the US, UK, Egypt and Vietnam. I favour transparent, explainable methods over black boxes, and I work best in teams: every paper I have written is collaborative, across faculty and institutions.

What I believe06 / 06

Accounting numbers are promises to the public.

Technology should make those promises easier to verify, not easier to fake. I believe rigor comes before speed, that ethics is part of measurement rather than an afterthought, and that good research should be clear enough for a practitioner to act on.

Research agenda

My work so far follows two empirical threads, connected by a methods interest in explainable and verifiable AI.

ASC 820 · ASC 350-60

Digital assets and fair value

How firms choose a principal market and pricing source for crypto assets now measured at fair value, and how reliable those measurements are.

ESG · mandatory disclosure

ESG disclosure and markets

Whether mandatory disclosure changes how markets react to ESG news, including news that spreads on social media.

XAI · blockchain · LLMs

Trustworthy AI in accounting

Explainable machine learning for prediction, and verifiable pipelines that make AI outputs auditable before people rely on them.
