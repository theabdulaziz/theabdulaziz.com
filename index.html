Principles02 / 08

Reliable information is the foundation of good decisions.

Accounting matters because people rely on it. I believe new technology should make reported information easier to verify, that ethical judgment is part of measurement, and that research should be rigorous first and clear enough for practitioners to use.

The PhD03 / 08

Accounting and Operations, studied through Analytics and AI/SI.

I am interested in how firms measure and report information that is difficult to value (digital assets, ESG performance and AI/SI-generated outputs) and how auditors, investors and managers use that information when it is uncertain.

Research Direction04 / 08

Approach05 / 08

As a graduate research assistant I led the US data collection for a four-country ESG study, building firm-year panels from Bloomberg and annual reports. I prefer transparent methods whose results can be explained, and all of my research to date has been carried out in collaboration with faculty.
